Privacy Policy

Devotional apps published by Ini · Effective 16 August 2026

In short: This app does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

The app shows advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. To do that, Google receives your device's advertising ID and technical information about your device. That is the only routine transfer of information out of the app.

This policy applies to the app you are using and to every Android devotional application published by Ini ("we", "us"). Those apps share a single design — bundled devotional audio and images, no account, no server of our own, and advertising supplied by Google AdMob — so one policy describes all of them accurately. Please read it before using the app.

1. Information we collect ourselves

None. The app has no sign-in, no user profiles, and no server of our own. We do not operate a backend, we do not receive analytics, and we cannot identify you. Nothing you do in the app is transmitted to us.

2. Information collected by Google AdMob

The app displays advertisements through Google AdMob, a service provided by Google. Depending on the app, these appear as a banner within a screen, as an occasional full-screen advertisement at a natural break such as moving between screens, and as an advertisement shown while the app is opening. Google acts as an independent data controller for this activity and collects the following automatically when an ad is requested or shown:

  • Advertising ID — a resettable identifier assigned to your device by Android, used to select and measure ads.
  • IP address — from which an approximate (city or region level) location may be inferred. Our apps never request or use GPS or precise location.
  • Device and technical information — device model, operating system version, language, screen size, network type, and similar diagnostics.
  • Ad interaction data — whether an ad was shown, viewed, or clicked, used for measurement and fraud prevention.

This information is handled under Google's own privacy policy, which we do not control: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Details of how Google uses data from apps that use its services are published at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Your advertising choices

Resetting or deleting your advertising ID

You can reset your advertising ID at any time, or delete it entirely so that apps can no longer use it, from your device settings. On most Android devices this is under Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by manufacturer). If you delete the ID, the app will still show advertisements, but they will not be personalised.

Consent (European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland)

If you are located in these regions, the app presents a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform before personalised advertising is enabled. Your choice is stored on your device and passed to Google.

You can change your choice at any time using the Privacy options button on the privacy policy screen inside the app, where that option is applicable to your region. If you decline personalised advertising, the app continues to work normally and shows non-personalised advertisements instead.

5. Information stored on your device

The app stores your advertising consent choice and standard Android app preferences locally on your device. If the app offers a ringtone, alarm or notification-tone feature and you use it, a copy of the bundled audio file is written to your device's media storage so the Android system can play it. Uninstalling the app removes its locally stored data; a tone file you have saved may remain until you delete it.

6. Children

These are general-audience devotional applications. They are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through one of our apps, please contact us and we will address it.

7. Data retention and security

Because we do not collect or receive personal information, we hold nothing to retain or delete. Information collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's own retention practices, described in its privacy policy.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of personal information, and to withdraw consent. Because we hold no personal information about you, requests concerning advertising data should be directed to Google, whose privacy policy explains how to exercise those rights. You may withdraw advertising consent at any time as described in section 3.

9. Third-party services

Our apps use one third-party service: Google AdMob (including Google's User Messaging Platform for consent). No other analytics, tracking, social media, or advertising services are integrated.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our apps change. The revised version will be included in the app update and published at this address, and the effective date will change. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
